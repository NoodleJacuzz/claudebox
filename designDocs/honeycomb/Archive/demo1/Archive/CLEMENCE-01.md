# Clemence, first kit (session 8) — ARCHIVED

Moved out of MECHANICS-01.md part 6 in session 9, when Noodle asked for her kit to be rebuilt around becoming Broken. The current design is MECHANICS-01.md part 7.2; this is the record of what was tried and why it did not work (her lust growth needed to be unreasonably high, Composure worked against her, Hallowed was weak, and Benediction cost a lot for little).

### Clemence — lust moved

**Primary: Confession.** Up to 6 Lust out of whichever ally holds the most, cast at an enemy with 3 more.
**Grace** (bar) fills one per 3 Lust her cards remove from anyone; **Benediction** spends 6. Heirloom the
**Rosary of Thorns**: 1 Composure for everyone at the start of each fight. Her lust tag is Exposure.

| Sister | Role | Rule | (I, S_v, S_h, C) | Outfit | Relic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Penance** | Offense | The party's lust cast onto the enemy | (5, 3, 4, 4) | **Inquisitor**: one Absolve becomes Lay Bare. Martyrdom blocked | **Confessional Grille**: enemies take +2 Lust from Exposure |
| **Martyrdom** | Utility | She carries it herself, and carrying it counts — even breaking | (5, 4, 4, 3) | **Anchorite**: +6 HP, one Rebuke becomes Take Their Sin, holds 8 more Lust before it counts. Penance blocked | **Hair Shirt**: every party member holds 5 more Lust before it counts |

**Her broken card is a blessing** (*Rapture*: ALL allies lose 5 Lust and heal 3) — Psychosis, the
top-scored Identity on the example list. She is the one member a player can mean to break, and *Ecstasy*
(rare) takes every ally's lust onto her at once.

Cards (14): Rebuke, Confession, Absolve, Vigil (starters) · Penance: Lay Bare, Scourge, Public Penance,
Transference · Martyrdom: Take Their Sin, Stigmata, Mortify, Hallow, Ecstasy.
