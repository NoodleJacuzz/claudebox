Sound library — measured

Every file in honeycomb sound/sfx, decoded and measured. Orange rows sound for longer than three and a half seconds, which is more than a card play can carry; blue rows are quiet enough at source that no trim can bring them up to the target, and want re-rendering louder.

idle.

Target loudness is written into the JSON as targetLoudnessDb; sfx-report.js reads it and derives each stem's trim from it. The measurement window and the 2% audibility floor are in this page's source.
