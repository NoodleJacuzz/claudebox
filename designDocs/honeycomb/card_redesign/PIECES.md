## New split pieces (2026-09-17)

Authored by Noodle; copied to `cardsGrok/` (originals kept in `_source/cards/`). **Back** pieces are
tinted by supertype; **front** pieces keep their gold. Full workflow in `PIPELINE.md`.

| Piece | Holds |
|---|---|
| `newribbon_back` / `newribbon_front` | ribbon cloth / gold trim |
| `newicon_back` / `newicon_front` | winged emblem / gold crest (covers the back's gold) |
| `newicon_priest` / `newicon_knight` / `newicon_lancer` | class glyphs (cross / sword / bolt) |
| `newring_cost_back` / `newring_cost_front` | cost coin blue well / gold rim + studs |
| `newring_owner_back` / `newring_owner_front` | owner coin blue well / gold rim |

Rings are loose sprites (cost 300×300, owner 250×250); ring splits are generated by
`overlays/splits/make-ring-splits.py`. Border (`border_blue`) and plate (`bg_priest_blue`) remain the
source rasters and are tinted with the back pieces.

## Visual notes (1b)

### `border_blue.png`

- 1b-1 Full-card rounded rectangle. Large inner hole. Outer rim ~electric blue; inner edge a darker bevel that follows the gold frame’s outer path.
- 1b-2 Opaque avg ~(7,121,199). Highlights go to white. Shadows to near-black. Top-centre sample (0,153,253).
- 1b-3 Soft outer glow / drop into empty alpha. Inner contact shadow against the gold.
- 1b-4 Brightest on the **top edge**, falloff down the sides (rim light from above).
- 1b-5 No lattice; solid enamelled rim.
- 1b-6 Specular ticks on the top rim, not a traveling gleam.
- 1b-7 Fringe 1–40: 364 px. Crop is clean-ish; inner bevel has mid-alpha (5441 px 41–200) — do not copy as muddy haze in step 6.
- 1b-8 Already its own file. Recolour in step 8, do not split.

### `frame_gold_horizontal.png`

- 1b-1 Landscape window. Inner hole **7.5% / 12.9% / 84.9% × 40.2%**. Filigree on all four sides; heavier at the four corners (scrolls + dots). Top-centre has a small arched rise under the gem slot.
- 1b-2 Gold-like pixels avg **(248,218,139)**; hi white; lo **(144,101,70)** copper. Inner bevel is **cool steel** ~(74–120, 87–164, 113–208), not gold — a blue-grey lip around the art.
- 1b-3 Filigree casts a short drop onto the hole. Inner bevel is an inset, not a drop.
- 1b-4 Warm gold brightest on upper-left scrolls; inner bevel brighter on the **bottom** lip (reflected fill).
- 1b-5 Filigree is vine/scroll, not a geometric hatch.
- 1b-6 Metal: white-hot edge on outer gold, warm mid, copper in recesses. Several highlight ridges per scroll, not one stroke.
- 1b-7 Fringe 1–40: 624 px (low). Mid-alpha 91. Crop is one of the cleaner pieces.
- 1b-8 Already gold on transparent. Do not split. Step 8 may recolour.

### `frame_gold_vertical.png` (basic placeholder)

Same top/left/right as horizontal; hole continues to **94.5%** down (H 81.5%). Same metal language.
In scope as the one extra piece for a placeholder vertical card. Not a full vertical design pass.

### `bg_priest_blue.png`

- 1b-1 **Not a full card.** Opaque bbox starts at y=810 (54%). Upper 54% is empty alpha. OT-1b: centre is **alpha 0** — art does not need a hole cut in this file.
- 1b-2 Avg ~(9,61,136). Hi (29,93,175), lo (5,39,100). Narrow range — a dim navy field.
- 1b-3 Plate sits inside the border; no extra drop.
- 1b-4 Slightly lighter toward the **middle of the plate** (under the ribbon / first lines of text), darker at the foot.
- 1b-5 Soft **diamond / lattice** in the navy, low contrast. Reads as cloth or engraved leather, not a loud grid.
- 1b-6 No metal sheen.
- 1b-7 Plate edge is a hard-ish alpha cut; some softness against the border.
- 1b-8 Already the lower plate. Do not split.

### `bg_necro_blue.png` / `bg_vamp_blue.png`

Same slot (lower plate). Necro avg ~(53,61,93), some near-white ticks — greyer, a little mist. Vamp avg ~(27,25,29), almost black. Same diamond idea, different value. Step 8 restyle if weak.

## Split list (step 2)

| Name | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `ring_owner_back` | `ring_owner` blue disc | behind the character portrait |
| `ring_owner_front` | `ring_owner` gold rim | clips portrait; metal shared |
| `ring_cost_back` | `ring_cost` blue disc | same split |
| `ring_cost_front` | `ring_cost` gold rim | same split |
| `ribbon_blue_back` | ribbon tails / under-fold | step-8 owner recolour |
| `ribbon_blue_front` | ribbon face + highlight | same |

Do not split: `frame_gold_horizontal`, `frame_gold_vertical`, `border_blue`, `bg_*`, `rarity_gold`,
`icon_priest`, `type_*`.

### Skip (do not copy / do not build)

- Type-colour frame sets (roster undecided)
- Extra exploded ribbon *shapes*
- Exploded JPGs as layers (fake transparency, duplicates)
- `type_unused`
- Source mistakes: paint-over smudges, leftover white matte, shadows baked on the wrong layer,
  dragged-edge layout shortcuts, crop wobble
- A full vertical design pass (the vertical **frame file** is not skipped)

### Wait (step 8)

- `icon_necro`, `icon_vamp`
- Owner recolour of border / ribbon / frame
- `type_random` (not this pass)

### Tags (not a raster)

School strip (`necromancy`, `Vampiric`, …) — not a supertype, not `type_*.png`. Lives at the foot,
behind the class icon. Current bench treatment: mauve chevron chip. Every card face must leave
room for at least one tag.

### To create later

- Type icons with a **thick outline** (not necessarily baked into the PNG — a stroke or CSS
  outline is fine) so they stay readable at small size when the ribbon is gone and the text well
  shows a large type icon.
