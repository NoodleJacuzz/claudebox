RANK 2. Same act as her venom 2. Different catalogue.

WHAT HAPPENS. She is watching futures and touching herself to them again, the same as her venom rank
2, but what she is watching has changed. These are not disasters any more. She has not asked herself
when the catalogue changed.

WHY IT IS A RANK 2. She knows what she is doing and she is doing it deliberately. The thing she has
not examined is what she is choosing to look at.

IMAGES. Three.
  1. Seated formally, on the clock, hands still.
  2. Hands no longer still, voice still ornate.
  3. Off duty, slumped, flat.

WORTH DECIDING. This scene and her venom 2 are the same act twice. Either that repetition is the
point and the two should be written to rhyme, or one of them should move. Your call.


HER VOICE, FOR REFERENCE

Cassadora has two voices. On the clock she is the classic fortune teller: ornate, slow, a
little theatrical. Off duty she is blunt and short. Switching between the two is the device every one
of her scenes uses.

She does not give in until rank 3. That is the one hard rule about her. Ranks 1 and 2 she is watching.

The world detail she carries well is what the futures will not show her. Her hints are shaped like
refusals, which is useful, because a refusal explains nothing.
