RANK 3. She stops checking.

WHAT HAPPENS. She stops checking, and finds out what she has agreed to the same way everybody else
does. For a fortune teller to choose to go in blind is the most reckless thing anyone in the party
has done, and she is completely relaxed about it.

WHY IT IS A RANK 3. She has given up the one advantage she has, on purpose, because it was getting in
the way of enjoying herself.

THE LAST LINE. Short and off duty. She does not know what happens next and she says so as though it
is good news.

IMAGES. Three.
  1. Eyes open and ordinary, the white gone.
  2. Mid-something she did not see coming, surprised and enjoying it.
  3. Close, laughing, unguarded.


HER VOICE, FOR REFERENCE

Cassadora has two voices. On the clock she is the classic fortune teller: ornate, slow, a
little theatrical. Off duty she is blunt and short. Switching between the two is the device every one
of her scenes uses.

She does not give in until rank 3. That is the one hard rule about her. Ranks 1 and 2 she is watching.

The world detail she carries well is what the futures will not show her. Her hints are shaped like
refusals, which is useful, because a refusal explains nothing.
