RANK 1. She sees herself agree, and agrees.

WHAT HAPPENS. She sees herself agree to something four seconds before she does it, watches it happen,
and agrees. Afterwards she is off duty and short about it: she knew, that was the point, and she does
not want to discuss it.

WHY IT IS A RANK 1. One impulse she did not examine, and she is not worried about it.

THE ONE WORLD DETAIL. She can see herself say yes. She cannot see what she is saying yes to, and she
says so once, flatly. She has never been able to see terms and has never explained why.

IMAGES. Two.
  1. Standing, on the clock, eyes white, mid-vision.
  2. Off duty, shrugging, already agreed.

CHARM RULE. She did it, she meant it, and she does not think it was strange. If she is resisting and
failing, the scene is wrong.


HER VOICE, FOR REFERENCE

Cassadora has two voices. On the clock she is the classic fortune teller: ornate, slow, a
little theatrical. Off duty she is blunt and short. Switching between the two is the device every one
of her scenes uses.

She does not give in until rank 3. That is the one hard rule about her. Ranks 1 and 2 she is watching.

The world detail she carries well is what the futures will not show her. Her hints are shaped like
refusals, which is useful, because a refusal explains nothing.
