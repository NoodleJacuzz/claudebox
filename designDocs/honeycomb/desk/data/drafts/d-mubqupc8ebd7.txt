RANK 3. She goes and becomes one of them.

WHAT HAPPENS. She goes back down alone and becomes the version of herself she has been watching.
Before she goes she checks one last time, sees herself decide to go, and then stops checking.

WHY IT IS A RANK 3. Same shape as your Nettle rank 3. She talks herself through it, gives up, and
acts. The difference is that the person she loses the argument to is a version of herself she has
already watched lose it. She knows exactly what she is walking into.

LUST BATTLE. Venom rank 3 is the natural place for one. She stays dressed the whole time, so she can
have one where Brienne cannot.

IMAGES. Not settled. Three is the guess.


HER VOICE, FOR REFERENCE

Cassadora has two voices. On the clock she is the classic fortune teller: ornate, slow, a
little theatrical. Off duty she is blunt and short. Switching between the two is the device every one
of her scenes uses.

She does not give in until rank 3. That is the one hard rule about her. Ranks 1 and 2 she is watching.

The world detail she carries well is what the futures will not show her. Her hints are shaped like
refusals, which is useful, because a refusal explains nothing.
