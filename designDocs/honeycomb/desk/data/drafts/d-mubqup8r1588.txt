RANK 1. She sees it coming and does not move.

WHAT HAPPENS. She sees the splash coming about four seconds before it happens, and stands there and
lets it hit her. Afterwards somebody asks why she did not move. She is off duty by then, so the
answer is short and unhelpful: it happened in most of the futures, so there was not much point.

WHY IT IS A RANK 1. She is not worried and the player is. She has already decided this is going to
keep happening to her, and she finds being asked about it tedious.

THE ONE WORLD DETAIL. She can see what happens next but not what the stuff actually is. She says so
once, flatly, and does not explain. The line is worth having because it refuses to answer anything.

IMAGES. Two.
  1. Standing in it, on the clock, ornate and unbothered, eyes closed.
  2. Close up, off duty, flat expression, wiping her face.


HER VOICE, FOR REFERENCE

Cassadora has two voices. On the clock she is the classic fortune teller: ornate, slow, a
little theatrical. Off duty she is blunt and short. Switching between the two is the device every one
of her scenes uses.

She does not give in until rank 3. That is the one hard rule about her. Ranks 1 and 2 she is watching.

The world detail she carries well is what the futures will not show her. Her hints are shaped like
refusals, which is useful, because a refusal explains nothing.
