## Slot

**Relic** — any character. **Heirloom** — one character. **Equipment** — teambuilding gear, same modifier shape.

| | Relic / heirloom | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Benefit | Medium | Low = node. High = outfit |
| Allowance | One pick (more only in a small party) | Forever-on unlimited = node. One worn identity = outfit |
| Feel | Glad to have, interchangeable | Decides Burst vs Grind. Sister switch. Starting-deck rewrite |
| Common | Unlock on obtainment; equip from then on | |
| Rare | Vanishes when the run ends | Live relic `uncommon` **is rare**. No middle rank. |

**Lives here:** survive-lethal once; Super Exhaust; Weight Training; party-wide friction dampen; resource refunds (or outfit); T1 stabilize if it is not the Opening exclusive node.

**Does not live here:** category banishes; sister weighting; A2-as-build-around; engine scaling; starting-deck identity; extra copy of an outfit's whole rule.

## Heirlooms (start worn)

### Iron Sigil — Brienne, common
Party: 4 tHP each at combat start.
**KEEP.** T1 shell, party-wide, interchangeable. Overlaps the T1-buffer node at a different scale (party 4 vs per-character 5).

### Shadow Locket — Nettle, common
+1 energy on turn 1.
**FAIL.** Too generic, does not fit Nettle's gameplan. Early design that should be changed to rare artifact.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*, rare relic): +1 energy on turn 1, any party, vanishes at run end.

### Crimson Fang — Severine, common
Enemy downed: all allies heal 3.
**KEEP.** Interchangeable sustain. Same job as Pilgrim's Bell; two is OK if numbers stay small.

### Ember Spur — Cinder, common
Cinder starts at the back.
**FAIL.** Player reorders freely; the spur does not stick.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): The first debuff she gains each combat: gain 2 Stride.

### Votive Candle — Clemence, common
When an ally Breaks, each other ally heals 10.
**KEEP.** Heal is situational and does not advance the state. 10 on a Break is not a damage equivalent.

### Crystal Ball — Cassadora, common
Draw 1 extra on turn 1.
**KEEP.** T1 stabilizer. Same family as Traveler's Lantern; hers is character-bound.

## Generic equipment

### Whetstone — common
+2 damage on the first attack each turn.
**FAIL.** Reaching critical mass of +1 damage effects that would make Vex's and future multi-hit moves busted.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): Attacks that hit **exactly once** deal +3.

### Heavy Pauldrons — common
+8 Max HP. tHP gained −1.
**KEEP.** Weight Training shape (early HP, worse gold). Lives here, not on a forever node.

### Thief's Gloves — rare
Adds Second Wind to the deck; grants a Second Wind ability.
**FAIL.** Starting-deck rewrite + a button. That is outfit / signature space. Draw on a loadout pick is a carry. Second wind is not exciting enough to earn a deck slot in current status, feels more like a nerf to include in the deck.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): Combat start: if your deck has 20 or more cards, draw 1.

### Duelist's Blade — Brienne, rare
Sword Strike becomes Riposte.
**FAIL.** Armament identity on an item. Tree node Keen Edge already did this. Dies with the new basics anyway.

## Relics — generic

### Bone Pendant — rare
Draw 1 extra **every** turn.
**FAIL.** Forever draw is not a safety net. It removes the hard part of every turn. T1 draw (Lantern, Skull) stays; this does not.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): The first time each combat your hand is empty, draw 2.

### Gilded Ledger — common
+15 gold per combat.
**WATCH.** Economy was parked on **nodes** because the relic slot is too dear for a gold drip. Per-combat 15 is bigger than starting-gold +5 — either it earns the slot or it becomes a Bounty node. Not both.

### Crackseal Wax — rare
First debuff on each ally negated (Artifact 1).
**KEEP.** Party-wide first-cleanse. The node is per-character; this is the Medium party version. Rare, so it should vanish at run end.

### Wardstone — common
+2 tHP whenever anyone gains tHP.
**FAIL.** tHP engine scaling. Leads to complex scenarios where edge cases will confuse players.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): The first tHP gained each combat is +6.

### Traveler's Lantern — common
Draw 1 extra at combat start.
**KEEP.** T1. Same job as Crystal Ball, any party.

### Pilgrim's Bell — common
Enemy beaten: most-hurt ally heals 4.
**KEEP.** Interchangeable. Stacks with Fang — watch the two together.

## Rare heirlooms (character in the party, vanish at run end)

Live `uncommon` + `offerCondition` relics belong here. Uncommon is out of date design terminology.

### Gilded Gauntlet — Armament, rare
Attacks +1 dmg per 8 tHP the attacker holds.
**KEEP.** Reads tHP on anyone. Not hybrid friendly, only one character consistently builds tHP -and- attacks.

### Oathbound Banner — Sentinel, rare
tHP absorbs a hit: 2 dmg to the attacker.
**KEEP.** For clarity, this is essentially just conditional thorns

### Collection Plate — Tithe, rare
First tHP spend each turn: draw 1.
**FAIL.** Way too specific, free draw could be too busted, especially at common.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): First tHP spent each combat: most-hurt ally heals 4. No draw.

### Cracked Ampoule — Rupture, rare
Poison +50% vs Weak.
**REPLACE.** Cross-party (Cassadora/Severine Weak). Small. Overall good, but poison deals damage in small numbers, no 1.5 damage.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): Weak enemies take +1 Poison damage per tick.

### Rat King's Bell — Contagion, rare
Poisoned enemy falls: others gain half its Poison.
**WATCH.** Contagion payoff. Hybrid with little poison gets little. Borderline High.

### Honeyed Thorn — Venom, rare
Poisoned enemies take +2 Lust from everything.
**FAIL.** Venom payoff. Triples poison lust damage, way too strong.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): The first Lust each turn on a poisoned enemy is +3.

### Trophy Cord — Feast, rare
Debuffed enemy falls: draw 1.
**KEEP.** Any debuff. Refund, once per kill, some encounters only have one enemy, situational.

### Leech Jar — Bloodletting, rare
First time each turn the party damages itself: +1 Energy.
**WATCH.** Energy refund. The Bloodletting engine. Same rule as Collection Plate.

### Vitae Chalice — Transfusion, common
Ally healed: also soothe 2.
**WATCH.** Potentially triggers from too many effects.

### Spur of Embers — Charge, rare
Front ally's attacks +2.
**FAIL.** Critical mass of stacking damage buffs, effectively applies to the entire party.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): The ally at the **back** deals +2 with attacks.

### Marching Drum — Formation, rare
First 2+ place move each turn: draw 1.
**FAIL.** Too easy to trigger for some party compositions, impossible for others, complete feast or famine.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): When Cinder moves 2+ places, the ally she passes gains 3 tHP. Once per turn.

### Prayer Beads — Devotion, rare
First self-Lust from own card each turn: +1 Energy.
**REPLACE.** Clement's archetypes reworked.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): First time each turn Clemence heals an ally, that ally soothes 2.

### Reliquary of Tears — Rapture, rare
Ally Breaks: all enemies take 6 Lust.
**KEEP.** Payoff for a thing that already happened. Not an engine.

### Halo of Thorns — Sanctuary, rare
Broken allies heal 4 at turn start.
**KEEP.** Holds them without standing them (heal, not soothe). Safety net.

### Cracked Hourglass — Hex, rare
Intent changed: draw 1, at most twice a turn.
**FAIL.** Free accessible card draw, triggers off ability.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): First intent change each combat: Cassadora gains 1 Foresight.

### Copycat Quill — Repertoire, rare
Stolen moves don't exhaust; a copy stays in the deck.
**FAIL.** That **is** the Soothsayer outfit passive. Relic must not grant the sister's whole rule.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): Stolen cards cost 1 less this combat. They still exhaust.

### Sleight Purse — Turncoat, rare
Combat start: one enemy move in hand, 0-cost, exhausts.
**FAIL.** Makes stealing cards not special enough, too random to actually be useful, bloats Grifter deck size.
**Potential new effect** (*draft*): Combat start: see all enemy intents. No card.
