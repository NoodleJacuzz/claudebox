# OUTFITS-LIST — working list

Lean. Brief: `../STARTER-REWORK-01.md` §1.4. Art: `../../art_pipeline/OUTFITS.md`. `—` = not written.

One worn. Default from start. Three alts: run-win / shop / tree-end.

The draftable pool is already split by sister mechanic (`MECHANICS-01` part 5). Lists below are **reminders for a later pool rework**, not a lock plan. Player-facing boost/block names the cards or a plain effect, never the sister's system name.

---

## What each outfit must have

**Default:** no signature (no mechanic to be emblematic of yet). Adds **3 random legal cards** from this character's drop pool, rolled once at run start. Passive: **+20% personal XP**. No boost, no block.

Until the pool pass gives default its own sister, do not invent a signature by stuffing a graduated common back into the starter.

**Alt:**

| Slot | What |
|---|---|
| Signature card | 1 in the opening deck. Emblematic. Not a carry. No 0-cost draw/energy. **Does not appear in rewards.** |
| Boost | This sister's cards appear more. |
| Block | The opposing sister's cards appear less / never. |
| Gated | *Potential* lists only. Target ≥3 commons and ≥2 rares when the pool is reworked. **Unlocked rare** (any pool once the outfit is unlocked): synergy with other characters' cards. **Worn-only rare:** selfish to this sister. |
| Passive | A rule that makes Act 1 feel like this sister. Needs a drawback. |
| Drawback | HP, a tax, the blocked sister. |
| A2 | Optional. This outfit makes A2 the build-around, or replaces it. |

HP +5 ranks is a **node**. Outfit HP is the identity swing, not another +5.

Old starter replacements (Tithe→Riposte, etc.) die with the new 2+2+signature deck.

## Brienne

tHP is a weapon / tHP is a wall / tHP is currency.

### default

Unlock: from start
Adds: 3 random legal from her drop pool (run start)
Boost: Unobtained cards
Passive: +20% personal XP

### siegeplate — tHP is a weapon

Unlock: —
Signature: Plate Edge — Deal 4. Gain 4 tHP.
Boost: tHP-as-damage cards
Reduce: taunt / intercept cards
Potential gated commons: Shield Bash, Riposte, Tempered Plate, Lend Steel
Potential gated rares: Crushing Weight; Unstoppable (worn-only)
Passive: Brienne's tHP is reduced by attacks only *after* her base HP reaches 0.
A2: Spend all Resolve. Deal that much to the front enemy.

### bastion — tHP is a wall

Unlock: —
Signature: Headstrong — 3 Cost, Deal 18 damage, costs 1 more each time you lose HP this battle.
Boost: tHP / taunt / intercept cards
Reduce: spend-tHP cards
Potential gated commons: Challenge, Intercept, Thorn Armour, Iron Retort, Stand Fast
Potential gated rares: Shield Wall (worn-only)
Passive: Gain resolve twice as fast, resolve only increases when you lose tHP.
A2: Disabled. Instead, once per battle, if Brienne would break immediately lose all resolve and gain half that much tHP.

### almoner — tHP is currency

Unlock: —
Signature: Incredible Wealth — 0 cost, spend all your tHP, your next card's cost is reduced by 1 for each 5 tHP spent.
Boost: spend-tHP cards
Reduce: tHP-as-damage cards
Potential gated commons: Tithe, Ransom, Gilded Strike, Alms
Potential gated rares: Reliquary (worn-only)
Passive: Brienne loses all tHP at the end of her turn, draw an addition card each turn.
A2: Spend all Resolve. Each other ally soothes 4 and spends 4 tHP if they have it.

## Clemence

Note: While Cinder, Cassadora, and Vex have promise, they were improperly designed leaving them not enough room for four well-defined builds.
All three of the previous mechanics just describe parts of Clement's core game plan, and isolating them did not leave her with a complete toolkit. Healing allies and *wanting* to gain lust is her core identity, all while putting the party at risk of game over afterwards due to pushing them towards broken herself.

Healing, Make Breaking Difficult / Rushing towards self-break / Breaking allies.

### default

Unlock: from start
Adds: 3 random legal from her drop pool (run start)
Passive: +20% personal XP

### devotee — Healing, Make Breaking Difficult

Unlock: —
Signature: Blessed Pain — 2 cost, An ally heals 20.
Boost: Healing cards
Reduce: Self-Lust cards
Potential gated commons: Unknown since the rework changed archetypes
Potential gated rares: Unknown since the rework changed archetypes
Passive: Prevent all self-inflicted lust.
A2: Spend all devotion, give tHP equal to the amount lost split across allies.

### ecstatic — Rushing towards self-break

Unlock: —
Signature: Edge — 0 cost, you gain 6 Lust.
Boost: Self-Lust cards
Reduce: Sanctified / ally-Lust cards
Potential gated commons: Unknown since the rework changed archetypes
Potential gated rares: Unknown since the rework changed archetypes
Passive: Clemence cannot be soothed. Lust gained while broken is randomly distributed among allies and enemies instead.
A2: Spend all devotion, gain that much lust.

### abbess — Breaking allies

Unlock: —
Signature: Guided Hand — An ally gains 2 strength and 6 lust.
Boost: Sanctified / ally-Lust cards
Reduce: Healing cards
Potential gated commons: Sanctify, Fallen Vigil
Potential gated rares: Communion (worn-only)
Passive: Immune to lust inflicted by enemies.
A2: Spend all devotion, each other party member gains that much lust.
