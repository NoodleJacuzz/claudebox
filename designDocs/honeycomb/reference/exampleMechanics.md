Adapt - User is presented with three options from a pool of nine buffs, chooses one, and applies it to a friendly target.
Affinity for X - Card costs less based on how much X you have.
Build-a-card - Make several choices on the cost and affect of a custom card before adding it to your hand.
Combo - Some kind of bonus if the controller has already played a card this turn.
Living Dangerously - Shuffle a negative effect into your deck.
Bullet - Shuffle lots of a specific token card into your deck.
Mixture - When two mixtures are in your hand together, combine them (but keep the original cost).
Corpse - Resource gained whenever an ally dies.
Motivate - Upgrades in hand after you play a card which costs more than this one.
Counter - Disables the card your opponent was about to play.
Customize - You can click on this card outside of battle to change its effects.
Memento - Does something when the card's owner dies.
Generate p - Choose one of three cards to add to your hand.
Generate a(n) X - Choose one of three cards (from a pool defined by X) to add to your hand.
Protection - Blocks the next instance of damage.
Delay - Does not activate immediately, instead activates after several turns.
Discard - Discard a card from your hand.
Double/Triple - You can use this card two/three times in a single turn.
Drain - Restores life equal to damage dealt.
Fish Up - Look at the bottom 3 cards of your deck, choose one to put on top.
Sink - After activating, put a really good card at the bottom of your deck.
Echo - Returns to your hand after playing so you can re-play it for its cost.
Enrage - Empowered based on missing health.
Excavate - Add a random card from a pool of treasures to your hand. Each time you excavate, the rarity of the treasure obtained increases.
Partner - When added to your deck, add complimentary companion cards as well.
Finale - Empowered if this spends the last of your energy.
Forge - You can spend a small amount of energy to empower this card for when you eventually cast it later.
Shift - Transforms this card in hand base on a characteristic such as the last card played.
Revenge - Empowered if the owner took damage since their last turn.
Fusion - Can be played alone, but if dragged onto a partner card they combine into a new one.
Desperation - Empowered if you have few cards in hand.
Hero - Replaces the user who played this card with a new character with their own abilities.
Highlander - Empowered if you have no duplicate cards in your deck 
Exact Damage - Empowered if it does exactly lethal damage.
Blessing - Enhances the character's personal activated abilities.
Parasite - Upgrades in hand after X damage is dealt while this is in hand.
Inspired - Empowered if the owner used a personal activated ability this turn.
Family - Empowered for each ally with a specific tag.
Minion - Summon an entity less useful than a full player character, but still able to act in combat.
Movement - Change the character's position in the party (usually in ways other than the default of offense moving to the front).
Prime - After playing this, shuffle an empowered version into your deck.
Prophecy - You can spend energy to banish this card to be cast later, sometimes for a different cost or effect.
Edge - Empowered if this was played as the left or rightmost card in your hand.
Overheal - Empowered if this card would heal a character over their max HP.
Exceed Limits - Controller has less energy next turn.
Quest - Lingers on the battlefield creating a sub-objective for a reward.
Quickdraw - Empowered if played on the same turn it was added to your hand.
Ramp - Gain more energy permanently.
Return - Return this card to your hand from the discard pile when you meet a condition.
Rhythm X - Empowered if this is the Xth card you've played this turn.
Scaling - Gain an incremental power boost each time this /associated cards are played.
Secret - The player can't see what this card will do.
Silence - Removes buff effects.
Start of game - While this is in your deck, do something at the start of the game.
Spellboost - Empowered whenever you use a card of a certain type while this is in hand.
Stance - Transitions the character between one of multiple exclusive states.
Stealth - Makes something untargettable by cards and abilities while allies are still alive.
Taunt - Redirects attacks and damage.
Theft - Steal the effects of your opponent"s card.
Tradeable - Drag the card back over your deck and pay a cost to shuffle it back in and get some reward.
Tutor - Draw a specific card or type of card from your deck.
Wish - Choose a temporary copy of any legal card to add to your hand.
X-cost - Spend all of your energy to cast this, do something for every energy spent this way.
Overheat - Still playable when you can't afford the energy cost, but if you do ends your turn and burns the card into an unplayable curse card afterwards.
Retain - Stays in your hand at the end of your turn.
Junkmaster - Empowered if you have any unplayable curse cards.
Garbage fire - Exhausts curse cards in your hand.
Risk - Increases the effectiveness of enemy cards.
Martial Arts - Empowered based on if the card caused you to change position.
Gacha - Summon random individuals from across the roster of enemies to playable characters to bosses
Sadism - Deal damage to friendly units or self.
Masochism - Empowered if damaged by self or friendly units.
Enchant - Grant yourself or allies personal activated abilities that can be used freely
Balance - Empowered if the number of cards in your deck is even.
Cruelty - Empowered if the target has a negative status effect.
Frenzy - Card activates for free when discarded.
Psychosis - Card has a positive effect when broken instead of the usual effects.
Cheerleader - Draws cards that belong to allies.
Enhance - You may pay an additional X to empower this card when you play it.
Lone wolf - Empowered if you have no party members.
Flashback - This card can be played from your discard pile.
Greed - Empowered if you have a card too expensive to play in your hand.
Archeology - Empowered if you have a certain number of relics.
Fashion - Card effect changes based on costume worn
Repeat X - When played, replay this card X times.
Arduous - Inflicts a strictly negative status on the card's owner.
Resistance - Empowered if you have a strictly negative status effect.
Transmogrify - Changes strictly negative status effects into positive equivalents, and vice-versa.
Berserk - Status effect causing affected to use cards for free on random legal targets.
Goad - Status effect giving a boost to damage but only allowing the use of attack cards.
Freeze - Status effect where the affected wastes their next card played unless they pass the turn without playing, but also decreases damage taken.
Burning - Negative status effect that deals a small amount of core HP damage X times to the affected before cutting X in half before their turn starts, where X is the stacks of the status.
Regen - Positive status effect that restores a small amount of core HP X times to the affected before cutting X in half before their turn starts, where X is the stacks of the status.
Fracture - Status effect that deals a single instance of damage multiplied by X to the affected before cutting X in half before their turn starts, where X is the stacks of the status. Damages temporary health first.
Distracted - Negative status effect that inflicts X lust at the *end* of the affected's turn before cutting X in half, where X is the stacks of this status.
Steaming Body - Positive status effect that inflicts lust to all enemies at the end of the affected's turn.
Mounted - Positive status where the affected rides a mount, gaining temporary HP and new cards based on the mount, lost when temporary HP runs out.
Opener - This card always starts in your hand.
Opening act - The characters core theme relies on an activated opener card.
